Game and purchase support

Dig for resources, dodge hazards, and make it back to the surface. Upgrade your equipment to reach deeper ground on your next expedition.

If the full game or your skin pack is missing, sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy it, then restore purchases in the game. Game progress is saved on your device.

For help, email lando.idgamess@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, and what you saw on screen. You can also report a public bug on GitHub Issues.
